What's inside

Seven conversations. Scripted, so they actually happen.

1

The Guardianship Talk

With the person you are asking to raise your kids. Are they willing, are they able, and do they actually know what you are asking?

Without it: the person you named says no, because nobody ever asked them.

2

The Beneficiary Audit

The designations that quietly override your will. Check what every registered account and policy has on file, together.

The Funeral Preferences Conversation

So your spouse doesn’t have to guess at $15K. Religious or secular, music, the things you want avoided. Written down.

The one most people skip. Is your executor still willing, do they know where things are, and who is the backup?

Questions

Good to know.

We are a private family. We do not talk about this.

Most families don't, and that is exactly why the kit is written the way it is. You are not asked to open up about feelings. You are asked to check a designation, name a backup, and write down where something is kept. It is closer to sorting the insurance than to a heart to heart, and it is much easier to start when someone else has written the first sentence for you.

Is this just going to upset people?

The scripts are built to keep it practical, because a practical talk is a shorter and calmer one. You are not announcing bad news. You are asking whether the person you named is still willing, and what they would want to know if the day ever came. Readers tell us the relief comes fast, usually somewhere in the first page.

Do I need The Estate Organiser to use this?

No. The Kit stands on its own. It is one PDF and seven conversations, and it works whether or not you ever buy the book. The Organiser is where the paperwork gets recorded. The Kit is where the talking happens, and some of these conversations have to happen before the paperwork makes sense.

How long does it take?

About an afternoon for the first conversation, including reading the script. Most families do one at a time, when it feels right.

Is my information safe?

Yes. It's a file you fill in on your own computer, or print and fill in by hand. Nothing is stored online or in anyone's cloud, so there's nothing to be breached. Where you keep it is entirely up to you.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No, and that is by design. The kit helps your family talk, decide and write down what was agreed. For wills, nominations and legal documents, take what you decide to a lawyer or notary.

Does it work in my province?

Yes. The conversations apply anywhere in Canada, and where rules differ from province to province the kit flags it, like the Quebec rule that beneficiary designations belong in your will.

What exactly do I get?

Seven scripted conversations, the Legacy Letter Starter prompts, a 12-item Trigger Events watchlist, a 20-item Silent-Killer Audit, and a one-page Executor Prep Letter template. One fillable, printable PDF.
