## Which one to pick

- Filling it in on a computer: the Digital Edition.
- Want a book on the shelf that the family can find: Printed + Digital.

## Good to know

- The Estate Organiser is a record-keeping workbook. It is not legal, financial or tax advice and it does not replace a Will.
- What you write in it is never stored on our servers.
- Each country has its own edition. This page covers Canada only.
