## 2. Cut for lack of a source

Everything below was available in some form but did not meet the sourcing bar, and is
absent from the post. None of it was softened or hedged into the copy.

- **RDSP closure and the 10-year grant/bond repayment window.** The pack carries it
  (item 19) but flags it as reconstructed from a search summary of the ESDC issuer guide
  rather than a clean verbatim fetch of Chapter 8. Cut entirely: no RDSP content appears
  in the post.
- **The Chambre des notaires du Quebec register of testamentary dispositions, and the
  joint search with the Barreau du Quebec register.** The source page returned HTTP 403
  and the claim rested only on a search summary. Cut entirely. The post covers the
  Quebec will question through the verified notarial-versus-witnessed probate rule
  instead, and says nothing about a will register.
- **A general common law right of survivorship for joint bank accounts outside Quebec.**
  Every source for it was a law firm or funeral home page; the FCAC page fetched did not
  contain the text. Cut. The post states the Quebec rule (verified) and then says only
  that outside Quebec the treatment is a question for the bank and the province.
- **Quebec does not recognise TFSA successor holder designations.** Search summary only,
  never read on a live page. Cut.
- **RESP treatment on the death of a subscriber or beneficiary.** No quotable government
  page found. Cut.
- **Ontario's "Certificate of Appointment of Estate Trustee" as the name for probate.**
  The ontario.ca page returned HTTP 404. Cut; the post refers to Ontario's Estate
  Administration Tax, which is separately verified, and never names the Ontario grant.
- **The CRA hub page's own definition of "final return".** Not present in the fetched
  text. Cut; the post relies on the filing-deadlines sub-page, which was verified live.
- **Ontario's Estate Information Return 180-day filing requirement.** Seen on the live
  ontario.ca Estate Administration Tax page during the spot-check but not carried as a
  row in the source pack, so it was left out under the pack-only rule.

## 3. Notes on framing

- Quebec is treated inside four sections (registration, benefits, who is in charge and
  probate, registered plans and bank accounts) rather than as a footnote, and each time
  the post states plainly where the rule just described does not apply there.
- Ontario, British Columbia and Quebec are used as named examples of provincial
  variation. The Ontario and BC probate figures are framed "as at the date this was
  checked" and each links to its own provincial page. No province's rate is presented as
  national, and readers in other provinces are sent to their own.
- The two Canadian dollar rates quoted (Ontario Estate Administration Tax, BC Probate
  Fee Act) are the only provincial fee rates used, because they are the only two the
  pack marks verified with a live rate.
- Lawyers, notaries and advisors are framed throughout as worth paying for the legal
  corner. The disclaimer keeps "notary" for Quebec readers.
