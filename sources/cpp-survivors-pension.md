## 2. Cut or not verified

- A CPP contribution-years test for the survivor's pension. The CPP survivor's pension page does not state one (it says the amount depends on how much and how long the contributor paid in), so none is stated. The QPP test is stated because Retraite Quebec's page gives it.
- The QPP survivor's pension amounts. The Retraite Quebec survivor's pension URL tried (/en/citizens/death/survivors-pension) returned 404, so no QPP amounts are given.
- The flat rate portion as a dollar figure. Service Canada does not give it as a dollar amount on the pages read, so no figure is given.
