## Cut or not used

- The regional tax services office addresses: listed on the CRA page but long and region-specific, so the post points readers to the page instead of copying them.
- Holdback amounts: the CRA does not set one, so the post gives no figure and points to a professional.
- Provincial probate steps: vary by province and were not read today.
