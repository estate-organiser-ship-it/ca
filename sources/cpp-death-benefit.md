## 2. Cut or not verified

- The reel source's CRA hub line "A payment of up to $2,500" for the CPP/QPP death benefit. The CRA hub (doing-taxes-someone-died.html) still says this on 16 Nov 2026, but Service Canada's benefit page lists $2,500 plus a possible $2,500 top-up for deaths from 1 Jan 2025. The post uses the Service Canada page, which administers the benefit, and does not quote the hub.
- The payment-amounts table's "average amount for new beneficiaries" for the death benefit ($2,620.72). Left out: it sits beside a $2,500.00 maximum with a footnote, and quoting it without that context would confuse readers.
- The QPP five-year application limit as a bare "deadline". Retraite Quebec's page frames 61 days to 5 years as the window in which the first applicant is paid; the post describes it that way and does not call it a deadline.
