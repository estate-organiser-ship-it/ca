## 2. Cut or not verified

- The idea that an executor signs in to the deceased person's own My Account. No CRA page says this. The post says the CRA's route for an executor is Represent a Client, which the represent-deceased page states.
- Provincial unclaimed property programs and Bank of Canada unclaimed balances. Out of scope for this post and not re-read today, so not mentioned.
- The "Since February 2020, Canadians have cashed 5,826,170 cheques" line. Verified but cut for length.
